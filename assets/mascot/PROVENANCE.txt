SpaceEase beaver mascot

Prepared from the user-provided beaver.png character sheet using the built-in
Imagegen tool. These PNGs have transparent backgrounds and are bundled locally.

Prompts (shared specification):
Use case: background-extraction. Input image is edit target. Extract and reproduce
ONLY the specified pose from the supplied character sheet as ONE standalone app
mascot asset. Preserve its exact orange fur, cream belly, dark brown outlines,
flat softly shaded cartoon style, buck teeth, broad beaver tail and character
identity. Entire character centered, all tail and props visible, modest even
transparent padding. Actual transparent background. No other characters, no text,
no watermark, no extra scenery. Output a single square image.

Pose specifications:
- beaver-welcome.png: the waving beaver at top-left, friendly small smile,
  standing with one hand raised.
- beaver-coffee.png: the beaver holding a small cream coffee cup, calm and relaxed.
- beaver-notebook.png: the beaver holding the purple notebook and pencil,
  third row fourth column.

Usage:
- Welcome: greeting, paired with the app's purpose.
- Today: quiet companion next to the desk; hidden when the desk is hidden.
- Empty playbook: notebook, paired with guidance on what will appear here.
All poses remain static and decorative; they never judge an outcome.

Size: downscaled from 1254 px to 528 px (3x the largest use, 176 pt) with Lanczos
to keep the app light; the art is unchanged.
